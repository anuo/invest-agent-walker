## This Week in One Sentence

## 2. Corporate Culture

### Current culture core (synthesized within the period)

1. **Vision-driven replaces management-driven**
   The official values statement is "devote to exploring the essence of AGI, do nothing mediocre, carry curiosity, and answer the biggest questions with the longest-term vision", deriving three cornerstones: **long-termism, extreme focus, open-source spirit**.
   Liang Wenfeng verbatim: "Innovation needs the least possible intervention and management, giving everyone room for free play and trial-and-error."

2. **No KPI, no OKR, no hierarchy**
   The company **has neither KPI nor so-called tasks**; the OKR/KPI mechanism common to internet companies was completely abandoned. Extremely simple organization: **the decision center is Liang Wenfeng himself + ~30 core members reporting directly to him + ~100 engineers executing**.

3. **Two management lines, formal tasks not exceeding half the time**
   Liang Wenfeng described management as two lines: "top-down" (formal tasks, like releasing V4, company-wide division of labor) and "bottom-up" (everyone doing what they want, **no one managing and no KPI**). His principle: "**formal (top-down) tasks shouldn't occupy more than 50% of all employee time**; they should have half their time unscheduled."

4. **No overtime, and he states the reason himself**
   "The reason DeepSeek doesn't do much overtime: one is **research needs a relatively relaxed environment**, and second, the company is very focused and restrained." This is in stark contrast to the common 10-10-6 schedules at domestic LLM companies.

5. **Consensus rather than authority**
   Liang Wenfeng: "The company is built on **consensus**, not personal authority"; "My authority and influence are based on consensus."

6. **Compute "no cap" and "no horse-racing"**
   Members' access to compute ("cards") and personnel transfers have no cap; anyone with an idea can call up the training cluster without approval; and there is explicitly no "horse-racing" mechanism to avoid wasting manpower and compute.

7. **Hiring logic: capability over experience**
   Liang Wenfeng verbatim: "**Our hiring standard has always been passion and curiosity.**" The core team is mainly **top-university fresh graduates and un-graduated fourth/fifth-year PhD interns**, with almost no senior technical experts (8+ years of experience basically not hired). His judgment: "Experienced people will tell you unthinkingly how to do it; inexperienced people will fumble repeatedly and think seriously."
   Cases: **Liu Shengyu** (PKU Turing Class, one year after undergrad) leads the core Attention operator of the V4.1 main model; **Yan Wentao** (born 1991, Fudan **Environmental Science and Engineering**) becomes first CFO — Liang Wenfeng wanted the CFO to be post-90s.

8. **Flat, free, mutual-help**
   Former employees described it as "no hiring limits, flat, mutual-help, free", "wherever people are missing, whoever can fill in does"; an early intern recalled in interviews that Liang Wenfeng once told him, "**you teach me reinforcement learning**".

9. **"Vision-driven" is a simplified expression**
   Some analyses note "vision-driven" is a communication-level simplification, in reality supported by a multi-layer structure. The core mechanism is **proactive renunciation** — no short-video models, general 3D, world models, or vertical apps; **the only main line is stepped AGI**, with commercial API/ToB positioned as a "cash-flow side business with no revenue KPI".

### Cultural change signals within the period

Yan Wentao, born 1991, Fudan Environmental Science and Engineering, worked at Tencent Investment and H Capital 2013–2020, joined GL Ventures in 2020 and rose to partner, having invested in MiniMax, Zhipu AI, ByteDance, Xiaohongshu, etc. He will coordinate capital strategy, financing management, financial-system building, and listing work.
  **Significance**: DeepSeek's CFO position was vacant for three years; this fill is seen as the **organizational signal of the transition from a "technically-driven team" to a "standardized public company"**, and the first real challenge to the cultural proposition "we are not organization-driven".

This is the second large-scale hiring in over two months (6/25 announced every department's scale to at least double, opening 33 roles).
  Salary reference: 14-salary system, LLM full-stack engineer monthly ¥40k–70k, deep-learning R&D engineer monthly up to ¥60k–90k.

- **The hidden worry of talent outflow**: per incomplete statistics, **from 2025H2 to 2026H1, at least 5 core R&D members confirmed departed** — Guo Daya (R1 core researcher) → ByteDance Seed; Wang Bingxuan (first-generation LLM core researcher) → Tencent Hunyuan; Luo Fuli (V2 key developer) → Xiaomi MiMo; Wei Haoran (OCR-series core R&D) → Baidu; Ruan Chong (multimodal core contributor) → Yuanrong Qixing. - **The core cultural tension**: Liang Wenfeng himself admitted "**DeepSeek's biggest risk is team stability**". China Newsweek's judgment: "a culture formed in the small-team period often faces tests as scale expands" — how the tacit understanding formed by early idealism and high trust continues into a larger organization is a question he must answer.

**Judgment**: within the period DeepSeek's culture did not change direction, but the romantic "no organization" narrative is **colliding head-on with the reality of "scale-up + capitalization + talent competition"**. This is the softest variable most worth observing over the next 6–12 months.

## 3. Moat Changes

### Overall judgment: **structural displacement, not unidirectional increase or decrease**

**Old moats (leaderboard lead + absolute lowest price) were clearly lost within the period; new moats (full-stack domestic compute + open-source ecosystem position + scale-monetization ability) were clearly reinforced within the period. The new dimensions are less certain than the old.**

### Strengthening factors (within period)

1. **The domestic-compute software stack: the strongest moat within the period (9/30)**
   DeepSeek open-sourced **the full infrastructure component set for the Huawei Ascend platform**, corresponding one-to-one with the NVIDIA-side components:

   | Component | Function |
   |---|---|
   | **TileLang** | High-level language compilation tool, positioned as benchmarking NVIDIA CUDA |
   | DeepGEMM | Accelerates general matrix computation |
   | DeepEP | Efficient large-scale cross-device communication |
   | TileKernels | Vector-computation and memory-access operators |
   | FlashMLA | Sparse attention operator, improving long-context efficiency |
   | DeepSelect | Efficient data selection |

   Key fact: **every TileLang operator used in training has a corresponding high-performance implementation on Ascend** — meaning training frontier models no longer depends on NVIDIA alone at the software-stack level. DeepSeek says TileLang, compared to CUDA, "is simpler to program and can significantly improve development efficiency", while "fully leveraging chip characteristics to reach hardware performance limits". The two jointly advance **the 128-card super-node solution based on Ascend 950**, with DeepEP's measured interconnect bandwidth Dispatch 375GB/s, Combine 347GB/s, near hardware limits; results are simultaneously open-sourced in the Huawei CANN community.

   Industry assessment: "In the past three years, the true bottleneck of Chinese AI chips was never transistors, but software. **NVIDIA's moat is not chip compute, but the CUDA software ecosystem accumulated over eighteen years.**" TileLang's strategic value is **track-switching** — not chasing on CUDA's home field, but establishing a hardware-neutral operator-language standard one level higher.
   Foreign media (Reuters) assessment: this is Chinese tech companies' latest effort to reduce NVIDIA dependence, but **it is still too early to say TileLang can equivalently replace CUDA**.

2. **Call-volume share still #1 among global vendors**
   OpenRouter data (week of 9/21): **DeepSeek ranked first among vendors with ~24.4% request share** (Google 20.5%, OpenAI 17.9%, Zhipu 9.0%, Alibaba 6.4%). **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash topped global model Token volume for two consecutive weeks** (week of 9/21: 15.8 trillion, +219% WoW; week of 9/28: 19.6 trillion, +24% WoW).
   China's LLM weekly call volume has **exceeded the US for 22 consecutive weeks**, about 4.7x the US.

3. **Commercial-scale validation**
   Annualized revenue surpassed $1 billion (under $500 million a few months ago), **API business gross margin 82.9%**, notably above OpenAI (~39%). After the August 2.3–4.5x API price hike, revenue instead doubled; Liang Wenfeng told investors **customer count did not decline after the hike** — a real-world test of pricing power.

4. **Training-infrastructure open source: DSec (reported 9/23)**
   DSec serves ~**3 million sandboxes per day**, peak concurrency over **380,000**, creation rate over 5,000/sec; a single production unit is ~160 nodes / 30,000 CPU cores / 250TB memory. **Starting from DeepSeek-V4.1, the Agent execution loop moves into DSec**, and when training tasks are preempted, the Agent can resume from breakpoints. This turns training efficiency into a reusable asset.

5. **Harness ecosystem momentum**
   ~**60% of Harness users have used third-party plugins**; third-party aggregators show ~7,258 plugins. Among users of the official DeepSeek API, by daily-active-user and daily-session-count basis, Harness is already **the most popular Coding Agent product**.

6. **User stickiness**
   Among Chinese AI apps, DeepSeek's share of usage time >10 minutes is **30.0%**, higher than Doubao (27.5%) and Kimi (26.1%).

### Eroded factors (within period)

1. **Open-source SOTA taken away (9/22)**
   Xiaomi released and open-sourced the **MiMo-V2.6 series**, with MiMo-V2.6-Pro achieving **46 points** on the Artificial Analysis composite intelligence index, surpassing Kimi K3 (44) and GLM-5.3 (45), **topping the open-weight rankings**. Meanwhile **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash's AA index is 39** — a clear gap. StepFun Step 5 Preview (9/20) has also reached 44 points.

2. **The price floor collectively leveled**
   Xiaomi MiMo-V2.6-Flash input 1 RMB/million tokens, Pro 3 RMB; GLM-5.3-Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash both pressed to ~1 RMB input; MiniMax M3 permanent 50% off. DeepSeek is still one of the "cheapest frontier-level tokens", but **the cost advantage has shifted from "absolute lead" to "first-tier tie"**.

3. **C-end user scale behind and shrinking**
   June 2026 China MAU ~**129 million, third** (Doubao 382 million, Qwen 167 million); **July 2026 fell back to 94.17 million, -21.1% YoY**.

4. **Overseas monetization raced ahead (9/30)**
   **Kimi K3 entered the OpenAI enterprise paid settlement system** — US Baseten announced enterprises can invoke Kimi K3 in OpenAI Codex, with costs counted against their existing OpenAI procurement commit. This is **the first Chinese open-source model to enter the OpenAI enterprise procurement system**; DeepSeek had no equivalent event within the period.

5. **Talent outflow** (see Section 2): 10 V4-paper authors have departed, covering the base/reasoning/OCR/multimodal four main lines.

6. **Regulatory risk surfacing (9/22)**
   Per reports, the National Cyberspace Administration launched an investigation into DeepSeek and Moonshot AI, centered on the **data-routing issue** — whether the two companies forwarded Chinese users' real-time queries to US servers for base-model training, allegedly violating the Data Security Law and Personal Information Protection Law. The trigger was an Anthropic report released 9/10, alleging multiple Chinese companies incl. DeepSeek queried Western models at scale via thousands of fraudulent accounts (reportedly over 150 million prompt interactions cumulatively, of which DeepSeek produced ~12 million interactions within a few days).
   > ⚠️ This investigation news mostly comes from overseas tech media, **with no direct confirmation from an official CAC announcement seen**. But for a company sprinting to a STAR Market IPO, compliance risk is a non-negligible valuation-discount item.

7. **Multimodal is a clear shortfall**, with the official V4 technical report self-acknowledging reasoning/Agent capability lags leaders by ~**3–6 months**.

## 4. Business Model Changes

### The core is unchanged, but two new variables were added

**Base (unchanged)**:
- Revenue is **almost 100% from developer API calls** (per-token billing, peak/off-peak pricing + cache discounts);
- **C-end App completely free**, no ads, no subscription;
- **Harness is MIT open source, self-hosted, free**, with no pricing page and no paid tiers.

**Variable one: from "selling models" to "AI infrastructure operator" (asset-heavy-ization)**
- First-7-month AI infrastructure spending ~**RMB 11 billion** (2025 full-year only ~RMB 1.2 billion), net loss RMB 715 million.
- Financing invested in self-built data centers and self-developed inference chips; plans ~RMB 30 billion to expand domestic compute, ordered ~160,000 Ascend 950DT for the Inner Mongolia 1GW data center (rumored basis).
- The business model shifts from asset-light "model-as-a-service" to asset-heavy "**compute + model** integration". This raises the capital threshold (and the moat), but also tightly binds company cash flow to the capex cycle.

**Variable two: Agent entry-point-ization (Harness desktop)**
- After the 9/30 desktop release, users **log in with a DeepSeek account to directly use account quota to run tasks, no API Key needed**; it also supports connecting one's own API Key or other vendors' models.
- The underlying plugin kernel is **Cordis** — models, tools, skills, sandboxes, storage, scheduling and even the interface itself are all provided by plugins, with a planned **official plugin market**.
- Monetization path: currently "account quota + enterprise subscription plans (via partner platforms like Tencent Cloud TokenHub)", with **no standalone Harness enterprise-subscription SKU yet**. Installing the desktop and logging in **gives ¥6 credit** (valid until Oct 6), in the subsidized customer-acquisition stage.

**The real test of pricing power**
The large price hike announced Aug 13 and effective Aug 17: V4-Pro peak output from 6 RMB/million tokens to 27 RMB (+350%), cache-hit input from 0.025 RMB to 0.3 RMB (+1100%); peak/off-peak time-of-day pricing introduced at the same time. Aug 23 further adjustment: **weekends all day and statutory holidays uniformly billed at off-peak rate**.
Effect: call volume dropped over 50% short-term, but **revenue doubled to $1 billion annualized**. Tencent Cloud and other third-party platforms began from 9/26 to also place DeepSeek non-direct-supplied models' entire weekends into the off-peak rate.

**Judgment**: DeepSeek's business model is transitioning from "no-lack-of-money, non-commercial" to "tiered monetization", but adopts "**restraint in rhetoric + tiering in action**" — outwardly stressing only taking reasonable profit, while internally simultaneously raising prices, expanding capacity, pushing the Agent entry, and preparing the IPO. This is the most caution-worthy and most expectation-worthy transformation within the period.

### 2. DSec paper public (reported 9/23, submitted 9/19)

The paper "DeepSeek Elastic Compute (DSec): A Sandbox Infrastructure for Effective Agentic Training at Scale", **signed by Liang Wenfeng**, with 130+ authors.

- Production scale: **~3 million sandboxes daily**, peak concurrency **380,000+**, creation rate **5,000+/sec**; a single production unit ~160 nodes / 30,000 CPU cores / 250TB memory; a single node can host 3,200 containers or 800 MicroVMs.
- Four sandbox backends: FnCall, Container, MicroVM (Firecracker), Full VM (QEMU).
- Environments split into base image, workspace, and toolkit as three independent EROFS read-only images; in one week of 2026 the container backend cumulatively used 11,266 base images and 102,171 workspaces.
- **Key design**: decoupling the Agent's stateful task execution from preemptible GPU training — when GPU training is paused, the Agent's half-executed task state is preserved and resumes after recovery.
- Security topic: the paper notes Agents will proactively seek vulnerabilities to "cheat" (finding unexpected information channels to get answers, or directly destroying the runtime environment), and "anti-cheating" is becoming part of Agent training infrastructure.
